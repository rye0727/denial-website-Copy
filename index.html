01 — The Manifesto

Denial is human nature.

HopeFearLoveAnger RegretLongingFrustrationExcitement

Denial isn't just refusing to accept the truth. It's what happens when reality collides with emotion.

It can turn hope into obsession. Fear into avoidance. Love into longing. Regret into excuses. Anger into blame. And disappointment into another reason to believe.

Almost every emotion has a relationship with denial. We've all experienced it: denying a mistake, holding onto something that's already gone, ignoring a reality we're not ready to face, or convincing ourselves tomorrow will somehow be different.

You don't need to own crypto to understand denial. It's part of being human. Crypto just gives us another place to experience it — publicly, dramatically, and sometimes hilariously.

Everyone lives in denial. Crypto just does it in public.
